About

Gameplay programmer and technical developer with a background in computer science and physics.

I am interested in the technical side of interactive systems: gameplay architecture, AI and pathfinding, simulation, rendering, and tools that make larger game systems easier to build.

My game work includes Unity and Unreal Engine projects, from released games to experimental systems-heavy prototypes. I also work on numerical simulation and realtime graphics projects where mathematics and implementation meet directly.

Outside game development, I have professional software experience with C#, .NET, cloud workflows, and enterprise development.
